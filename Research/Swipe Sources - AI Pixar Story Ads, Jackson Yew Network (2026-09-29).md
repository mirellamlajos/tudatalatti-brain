Four sub-flavours of the same format:
1. **Meme-hook monologue with captions** (Jackson). Opens on a joke ("Business owners be like…"), then a first-person pain story.
2. **Narrated founder story with on-screen headline** (Brook). Third-person narrator, "This guy paid off $180,000…", enemy list, mechanism, proof stack, free-training CTA.
3. **Dialogue mini-movie** (Rise). No narrator. Characters talk, a friend reveals the product at 2:10 of 3:12.
4. **Advertorial narrator** (Health Insider). Long, health-claim heavy. Not a model for us.

Common habits across all four: vertical 9:16, polished Pixar look, one recurring character, burned-in captions, 2 to 7 minutes, product named late, story beats every 10 to 15 seconds, and a "Contains AI-generated imagery" disclaimer on some (Lasta, Zeely, Temu do this; Jackson does not).

No paid ad in the Meta library or GetHookd carries the phrase "Business owners be like" or "could never work a 9-5". The opener is an organic meme format. Inferred: Jackson swiped the visual format and the meme hook, not another advertiser's copy.

**What to copy from Brook, in order:**
1. **Hold the copy, vary the skin.** One primary text, 26 video skins in 5 days. Run our proven "law of attraction doesn't work" copy under three or four Pixar scenes before writing new words.
2. **The newsroom device.** A third-person anchor "reporting" on a person is a clean way to tell a founder story without the founder on camera, and it sidesteps first-person claims. For us: "A hír: egy 60 éves nő letett ezer módszert…" told by a Pixar anchor. Claims stay at *nyugodtabb*.
3. **Scene = proof.** Supermarket aisle, sauna showroom, Walmart. Each scene is the mechanism made visible. Our equivalent scenes: the bookshelf of failed self-help books, the kitchen table at 5 a.m., the reading moment.
4. **Headline card top, captions bottom.** Every frame carries a pinned headline ("$0 → $2M online. one product") plus rolling captions. Sound-off safe.
5. **Length.** 1:54 to 4:57. Long is fine in this format because the scene keeps changing.
