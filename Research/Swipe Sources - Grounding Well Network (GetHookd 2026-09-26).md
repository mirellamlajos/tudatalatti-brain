## Structural habits worth copying (seen across the set)

- **One-sentence opener with a number and a specific.** 26 years. 38 years of marriage. 1 in 12. A 94-year-old patient.
- **"Let me back up."** Almost every story uses the rewind after a shock open. It's the pattern.
- **The product is named late,** after the mechanism, often past paragraph 20. W-001 does the same.
- **Two beats never missed:** the "I tried everything" list and "the tests came back fine."
- **A witness confirms the change** before the narrator claims it (husband, daughter, guest).
- **A risk-reversal close.** Grounding Well leans on a 90-day money-back guarantee in nearly every ad. The brand has no guarantee on record; ask whether one exists or could.
- **Landing pages:** most go straight to the product page; the doctor ads go to journal advertorials (`/pages/bed-sheet`, `/pages/5-reasons`). So the advertorial page isn't a must for this format. Supports testing W-001's copy against the product page.
