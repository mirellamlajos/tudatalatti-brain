## Mining read (three detectors)

**Golden nuggets** (liftable almost verbatim, subject to the governors below):
- R03 *„a párom kisajátította magának és azt mondta, hogy ne szóljak hozzá míg Ő ezt olvassa"* (my partner commandeered it and told me not to talk to them while they read it). A vivid scene plus word of mouth. A strong hook or static.
- R05 *„mind ott vannak, csak valahol lent elbújva bennem"* (they're all there, just hidden somewhere deep inside me). Uses the brand's mechanism in the customer's own words.
- R10 *„Ezer féle módszert végeztem… blokkol valami ott bent"* (I've tried a thousand methods… something blocks me in there). The avatar's failed-solutions pain, now **real** (it replaces the composite [C] line).
- R13 *„végre valaki aki nem prédikál a pozitív gondolatok mantrálásáról"* (finally someone who doesn't preach chanting positive thoughts). Positioning validation. Possible brand echo (see the governors).
- R01 *„észrevehetően jobb a közérzetem, és kevésbé aggasztanak a mindennapi gondok"* (my wellbeing is noticeably better, and everyday worries bother me less). Experience language that's safe to use.
- R02 *„A legjobb magyar könyv amit valaha olvastam a témában"* (the best Hungarian book I've ever read on the subject). A social-proof headline.

**Messaging opportunities** (descriptors that recur across independent commenters):
- **"I stop, reflect, re-read."** R04, R06, R09 (3 of 15). *megtorpanok · elgondolkodok · visszaolvasok · vissza lapozok* (I pause, I reflect, I re-read, I page back). This clears the method's three-mention threshold, so it's a messaging-bank candidate: the book is read slowly, as a personal reckoning, not skimmed.
- **"Can't put it down."** R03, R11 (2 of 15), plus R05 "five chapters at once" and R06 "read it in two evenings." Close to the threshold.
- **Recommend-from-the-heart:** *szívből ajánlom / csak ajánlani tudom* (I recommend it from the heart / I can only recommend it) appears in 5 of 15. **Generic sentiment**, per the method's exclusion list. Not a nugget.

**Whole-review concepts:**
- **R05 (103 reactions, the most-reacted comment):** a full arc: hesitation → can't stop → recognising "my abilities were there all along" → strength to overcome sadness → "noticing the beautiful again." This is the review-as-primary-text play ([[Customer Review Mining Method]]; [[Advertising to Older Audiences]] format 7), run with a simple native image. Needs consent, and "szomorúság" (sadness) wording must avoid any depression claim.
- **R10:** trigger to action in one comment: tried everything → found the founder's videos → *„úgy érzem van remény"* (I feel there's hope) → ordered. It maps the TEEP journey ([[Emotional Delivery and Timing]]) in real words.
