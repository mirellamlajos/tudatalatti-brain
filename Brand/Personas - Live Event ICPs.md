# Personas — Live Event ICPs

**Audience correction (2026-09-25, CEO walkthrough, [Stated]):** these ICPs were built for the **live-event** campaign. **The book buyer is older.** The CEO puts the range at 30–65+, says most customers are 50+, and a big majority of those are 65+, many retired. Until the ad account's age and gender split is pulled, treat book and bundle ads as an **older-audience task** ([[Advertising to Older Audiences]]): plain words, longer stories, the founder as the expert, big text, calm pacing. The six ICPs still describe the *pains*; their age brackets describe the event crowd. In the CEO's words, buyers want better health, better finances and better relationships (including a loving partner); their brake is scepticism and unanswered questions; and they try to understand everything consciously. Full note: [[Brand Profile - Tudatalatti Kontroll]] → Who buys.

**Validation update (2026-09-24):** 15 real Facebook comments ([[Reviews - Facebook Comments (Book)]]) now support parts of this: the "tried everything, something blocks me inside" pain (core avatar and ICP 5), the anti-positive-thinking stance (ICP 5 sceptic), and a commenter base that seems to lean female (a name-based guess, low confidence). They add one thing the avatar missed: **practice friction**. Readers find the codes hard to keep doing, which is the natural bridge to the online course. ICPs 2, 3, 4 and 6 have no real-language support yet.

**Confidence note:** per [[Customer Review Mining Method]], these personas are **seeds, not validated personas**. ## Core avatar (the live-event buyer)

- **Age:** 28–52, core 32–45. **Gender:** 70% women / 30% men.
- **Location:** Budapest plus the big cities (Győr, Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs), and Hungarians in Austria, Germany and the UK.
- **Income:** employees on roughly 400k–1.2M Ft net a month; entrepreneurs and freelancers on roughly 1M–10M Ft a month, fluctuating.
- **Work:** office or management roles, HR, sales, marketing, healthcare, teachers, legal or finance, the beauty industry, coaches and mentors, e-commerce owners.
- **Identities:** *„Sikeresnek tűnök, de belül túlterhelt vagyok"* (I look successful but I'm overloaded inside) · *„Én vagyok a mindent megoldó"* (I'm the one who solves everything) · *„Fejben értem… csak nem csinálom következetesen"* (I understand it in my head, I just don't do it consistently) · spiritually open but anti-woo-woo.
- **Three pain clusters:** (1) self-sabotage and relapse, (2) constant tension, stress and mental noise, (3) relationship patterns and self-worth, including a money/self-worth block.
- **Emotional drivers:** wants control, but *„nem erőből, hanem belülről"* (from within, not by force); needs proof (*„mutasd meg, miért működik"*); is ashamed that someone so capable can't get it together.
- **Psychographics:** a mix of rational and intuitive (wants to understand *and* feel it); quality-sensitive, so will pay for depth and a system; wants community (*„nem egyedül"*, not alone).
- **Journey** (maps to TEEP in [[Emotional Delivery and Timing]]):
  - **Trigger:** *„Valami nincs rendben…"* (something isn't right)
  - **Exploration and frustration:** *„olvasok, videókat nézek… visszaesem"* (I read, I watch videos… I relapse)
  - **Evaluation:** *„kell egy reset, ami mélyebbre megy"* (I need a reset that goes deeper)
  - **Purchase and commitment:** *„Oké, ez rendszer… elkötelezem magam"* (Okay, this is a system… I'm committing)

## Cross-ICP truths (usable across all concepts)

- **Knowledge ≠ behaviour:** *„Fejben tudom… mégsem csinálom."* (I know it in my head… yet I don't do it.)
- **Permanent standby mode:** *„készenléti üzemmód"*, mental noise, can't switch off even when everything looks fine from outside.
- **The relapse cycle:** diet, sport, study, project, relationship → shame → start again.
- **Two camps on the spiritual side, believers and sceptics.** They need **two completely separate creative concepts** ([[Creative Strategy by Brand Size]] and [[Meta Creative Diversity (Andromeda v2)]]: different personas need visually different ads).

## Proof mix the research recommends

1. **Mirror with VOC** in one sentence using their exact words.
2. **Hint at the mechanism** ("not willpower: automatic patterns"), shown visually rather than explained.
3. **Community proof:** "you're not alone," especially for the shame cycles.

## Age and platform notes

The core is 32–45 with a strong 45–52 tail. The upper end of the age range fits [[Advertising to Older Audiences]] (longer story, authority, calm pacing). The founder himself suits that audience's trust shortcut ("the news are their influencers": a credentialed, plain-spoken expert). ICP 2 (18–35) needs the faster, younger register ([[Hooks]], age-based pacing).
