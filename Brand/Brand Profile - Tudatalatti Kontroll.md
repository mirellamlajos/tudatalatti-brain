# Brand Profile — Tudatalatti Kontroll

**Load order for any creative task:** this file → [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]] → [[Personas - Live Event ICPs]] → [[Language Bank - Founder and VOC]] → [[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]]. Together these are the "brand context" the Domain Knowledge docs require before producing output.

## What the brand is

A Hungarian self-development method built on one claim: **lasting change happens in the subconscious, not through willpower, positive thinking or motivation.** The subconscious runs ~90% of behaviour (brand-stated), it stores stress-created fear as *images*, and it can only be changed by (1) clearing those stress images and (2) sending new information as images in the alpha state.

It combines two established methods, credited openly:
- **Dr. Alexander Loyd — The Healing Code** (*Gyógyító Kód*) and *The Love Code* (*Szeretet Kód*): used for clearing stress images. Hungarian distributor and first official instructor: **Cserkuti Balázs**, a speaker at the brand's events.
- **José Silva — Silva Mind Control** (*agykontroll*): alpha state, visualisation, the "memory pegs" technique.

Full doctrine: [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]].

## Founder: Lajos Antal

- Architect-engineer turned entrepreneur, author and speaker. Has studied natural healing, law, philosophy and Eastern philosophy for about 30 years (as stated in the talks). Also presents Tenerife property investment at other events.
- Lives in **Tenerife** with his wife of 25 years. They moved there about 7 years before the Healing Code talk. Two daughters, whose achievements he uses as proof stories (see [[Stories and Proof Assets]]).
- **Stance:** an openly irritated critic of the American-style motivation industry: Napoleon Hill, Brian Tracy, *The Secret*/John Assaraf, "step out of your comfort zone," affirmations, vision boards. Signature reaction: *„kiráz a hideg"* ("these phrases give me the chills").
- **Voice:** warm, conversational, a bit meandering, self-deprecating ("my daughter says don't talk too long, it'll be boring"), full of personal anecdotes (his Mercedes, the Audi A8 on the M3, his daughters), and willing to be blunt. This is the founder-ad asset described in [[Creative Strategy by Brand Size]] and [[Creator Briefs]] (authorities do better with a looser brief).

**The ladder in one line:** Book 1 (understand) → Guide (apply) → Minikurzus (faster, deeper) → Online Course and live events (support and community). **Book 2** is the new front door for existing readers who got stuck at practice, which is exactly what the real reviews show ([[Interpreted Data]]).

**Brand identity after the rebrand (Aug–Sept 2026):** a deep red → royal blue gradient (Book 1 red, Book 2 blue), gold accents and buttons, and a gold swirl "figure" logo. The Hamvas quote sits in the footer as the brand line. The event visuals use a black and gold founder portrait with a signature.

The course's own positioning names who it's for: people who already have the book but feel they lack consistency, aren't sure they're doing the hand positions, coding or visualisation right, want real change in money, health or relationships, and want someone to "kézen fogjon" (take them by the hand).

## Positioning in one line

Not another motivational talk: **a system that works on the part of you that actually decides**, done inside your comfort zone, without willpower or hype.

- **Enemy:** the motivation industry and its "tévhitek" (false beliefs): willpower, persistence, hard work, positive thinking, affirmations, "success is a decision," "defeat is the gateway to victory," the Law of Attraction, burning desire, vision boards.
- **Mechanism:** see [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]]. This is the Unique Mechanism of the Problem and of the Solution in [[Scriptwriting]] VSL terms.
- **Tone line the audience wants:** *„Spirituálisan nyitott, de nem akar woo-woo-t – működő módszert akar."* (Spiritually open, but doesn't want woo-woo — wants a method that works.)

## Brand-type classification (for the headline and hook docs)

**Problem/solution brand** with a strong identity layer. The audience knows something is wrong ("elértem dolgokat, mégsem vagyok jól" — I've achieved things, yet I'm not okay) and has usually tried several fixes. So:
- **Market sophistication: HIGH.** They've tried meditation, sport, coaches, self-help, Dispenza and the Law of Attraction. Per [[Scriptwriting]] and [[Problem-Solution Headline Writer]]: don't over-educate the problem. Agitate failed solutions, lead with the mechanism, and frame it as a turning point.
- **Default awareness entry:** problem-aware / solution-aware ([[Killer Performance Ads]]). Their "failed solutions" are the richest hook territory ([[Hooks]] #2 Investment, #13 Controversy, #15 Myth-busting).
