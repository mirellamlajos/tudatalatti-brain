# Brand Profile — Tudatalatti Kontroll

**Load order for any creative task:** this file → [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]] → [[Personas - Live Event ICPs]] → [[Language Bank - Founder and VOC]] → [[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]]. Together these are the "brand context" the Domain Knowledge docs require before producing output.

## What the brand is

A Hungarian self-development method built on one claim: **lasting change happens in the subconscious, not through willpower, positive thinking or motivation.** The subconscious runs ~90% of behaviour (the talks) or 90–95% (the CEO's figure), both brand-stated; it stores stress-created fear as *images*, and it can only be changed by (1) clearing those stress images and (2) sending new information as images in the alpha state.

It combines two established methods, credited openly:
- **Dr. Alexander Loyd — The Healing Code** (*Gyógyító Kód*) and *The Love Code* (*Szeretet Kód*): used for clearing stress images. Hungarian distributor and first official instructor: **Cserkuti Balázs**, a speaker at the brand's events.
- **José Silva — Silva Mind Control** (*agykontroll*): alpha state, visualisation, the "memory pegs" technique.

Full doctrine: [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]].

## Founder: Lajos Antal

- Architect-engineer turned entrepreneur, author and speaker. Has studied natural healing, law, philosophy and Eastern philosophy for about 30 years (as stated in the talks). Also presents Tenerife property investment at other events.
- Lives in **Tenerife** with his wife of 25 years. They moved there about 7 years before the Healing Code talk. Two daughters, whose achievements he uses as proof stories (see [[Stories and Proof Assets]]).
- **Origin story (CEO walkthrough, 2026-09-25, [Stated]):** interested since his youth in Eastern philosophy, religion and self-help methods, the "big forces that shape a life." As an architect he cared about what's behind a building, where its soul is, what the crosses on sacred buildings mean. Then he read the Hungarian editions of the self-help canon (Napoleon Hill, Bob Proctor, even Donald Trump) and couldn't believe people bought it: it all stops at conscious decisions. His own life was the counter-proof: he'd decided things consciously many times and they still didn't turn out. Business ups and downs, **he almost lost everything and had to move the family to another country.** That's what pushed him past the conscious mind to the subconscious, and to Loyd's Healing Codes. Book 1 was written in **2018**; **online ads started in 2023.** The team calls him **Antal**. He **no longer wants to be on camera** (Sept 2026).
- **Stance:** an openly irritated critic of the American-style motivation industry: Napoleon Hill, Brian Tracy, *The Secret*/John Assaraf, "step out of your comfort zone," affirmations, vision boards. Signature reaction: *„kiráz a hideg"* ("these phrases give me the chills").
- **Voice:** warm, conversational, a bit meandering, self-deprecating ("my daughter says don't talk too long, it'll be boring"), full of personal anecdotes (his Mercedes, the Audi A8 on the M3, his daughters), and willing to be blunt. This is the founder-ad asset described in [[Creative Strategy by Brand Size]] and [[Creator Briefs]] (authorities do better with a looser brief).

## Positioning in one line

Not another motivational talk: **a system that works on the part of you that actually decides**, done inside your comfort zone, without willpower or hype.

**The middle ground, both ways (CEO, 2026-09-25):** the brand sits between two extremes and must never be read as either. Not the esoteric camp (angels, ghosts, demons, spirits that control us: never). Not the clinical camp that only accepts what a study proves. It never speaks against doctors or medicine ("medicine has saved countless lives"). Its line: *there are things bloodwork can't show and a pill alone doesn't fix*, the chronic-stress issues, and those are where the subconscious matters. It talks about the soul and emotions, but keeps them tangible. Most authors fall to one side; staying in the middle is the brand's ground.

**Mission in the CEO's words:** help as many people as possible reprogram their subconscious so they can live a more fulfilling, content and happy life. **Three pillars** everything is sold against: **health, relationships, wealth.**

- **Enemy:** the motivation industry and its "tévhitek" (false beliefs): willpower, persistence, hard work, positive thinking, affirmations, "success is a decision," "defeat is the gateway to victory," the Law of Attraction, burning desire, vision boards.
- **Mechanism:** see [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]]. This is the Unique Mechanism of the Problem and of the Solution in [[Scriptwriting]] VSL terms.
- **Tone line the audience wants:** *„Spirituálisan nyitott, de nem akar woo-woo-t – működő módszert akar."* (Spiritually open, but doesn't want woo-woo — wants a method that works.)

## Brand-type classification (for the headline and hook docs)

**Problem/solution brand** with a strong identity layer. The audience knows something is wrong ("elértem dolgokat, mégsem vagyok jól" — I've achieved things, yet I'm not okay) and has usually tried several fixes. So:
- **Market sophistication: HIGH.** They've tried meditation, sport, coaches, self-help, Dispenza and the Law of Attraction. Per [[Scriptwriting]] and [[Problem-Solution Headline Writer]]: don't over-educate the problem. Agitate failed solutions, lead with the mechanism, and frame it as a turning point.
- **Stage read (2026-09-30, Inferred): stage 4 moving into stage 5** on the five-stage scale in [[Market Awareness and Sophistication]]. Bare promises don't land; the mechanism has to be in the argument and enlarged (why the order matters, how little it asks); cold openings go to identification. Evidence, the one-belief sentence for each offer and the pre-filled Foundation Brief: [[Copy Foundations - Brand Application]].
- **Default awareness entry:** problem-aware / solution-aware ([[Killer Performance Ads]]). Their "failed solutions" are the richest hook territory ([[Hooks]] #2 Investment, #13 Controversy, #15 Myth-busting).

## Who buys (CEO, 2026-09-25; corrects the age picture)

- **Age:** 30–65+, but **most customers are 50+, and a big majority of those are 65+.** Many are retired, some still work. The live-event ICPs in [[Personas - Live Event ICPs]] were built for a younger event audience; for book ads, default to [[Advertising to Older Audiences]] until the account's age split says otherwise.
- **Why they buy:** better health, better finances, better relationships (including finding a loving partner). They've had a long life, tried many things, and nothing stuck. Chronic stress from their 20s and 30s is now showing up physically.
- **Why they hesitate:** scepticism ("will this really help me?") and too many unanswered questions.
- **How they think:** they want to understand every detail consciously, which is the opposite of what the book asks (let the subconscious do the work). Their language is simple and plain.
- **How they find the brand:** Meta ads first. Some see an ad, then Google the brand. Some come through friends and family. Meta is the main acquisition channel.

## Key messages and terminology (CEO, 2026-09-25)

**Key messages:** all change starts from within · the answer is in the subconscious · the subconscious controls 90–95% of our lives · we need to reprogram it · stop looking for solutions in the outside world; reprogram the inside and the outside changes.

**Terms the brand uses:** subconscious / subconscious mind · subconscious vs conscious (90 vs 10) · self-limiting beliefs · bad memories · chronic stress · fight-or-flight mode · how to change your life, your environment · healthier · more loving relationships · more financial abundance. The team's word for the method itself is **kódolás** (coding), confirmed by the brand's own ad copy ([[Winners - Ad Library]] W-001); used more internally than in ads. Hungarian equivalents and the founder's own phrasing: [[Language Bank - Founder and VOC]].

**Tone:** familiar, close to the audience, built so people recognise themselves in it.

**Proven angles (CEO-stated, no export seen yet):** *"the law of attraction doesn't work, do this instead"* is the strongest hook; *"change doesn't start outside, it starts within"* is a proven headline; images of Antal or the book work; book extracts work; the current scaling format is a native-style image with long storytelling copy.
