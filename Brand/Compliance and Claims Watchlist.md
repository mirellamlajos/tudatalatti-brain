# Compliance and Claims Watchlist

Every Domain Knowledge doc treats the brand's compliance framework as **"hardcoded walls, not guidelines."** Until the brand supplies its own, this is that framework. This is a self-development brand whose founder talks freely about illness, immunity and money, and those topics are where ads get rejected or create legal risk.

## 1. Health and medical: the biggest risk

The talks and the research say or imply that stress-image clearing affects the **immune system**, **illness** ("the anteroom of disease"), **panic disorder, sleep disorders, migraines, joint pain, skin problems**, and even **depression**.

- **Never in ads:** that the method, book or course **cures, treats, heals or prevents** any condition, or improves immunity. No symptom lists presented as results. No "instead of therapy or psychology."
- **Why:** EU and Hungarian unfair commercial practices rules (Hungarian Act XLVII of 2008, Annex item 17, prohibits falsely claiming a product can cure illnesses) and consumer-protection enforcement; plus Meta's health and wellness ad policies.
- **Safe reframes:** sensory and experience language ([[Making Iterations]] and [[Static Ad Recreation]] substitution libraries): "calmer evenings," "less mental noise," "I finally switched off," "stress management / self-development method," "what the book teaches."
- **The name "Healing Code" (*Gyógyító Kód*):** it's the product name of Loyd's method and can be named, but never pair it with a condition ("the Healing Code for migraines ✗").

## 2. Meta personal-attributes policy

Ads can't assert or imply that the viewer has a personal characteristic, including health, mental health, financial status or body weight.

| ✗ Don't write | ✓ Write instead |
|---|---|
| *„Szorongsz? Depressziós vagy?"* (Are you anxious? Are you depressed?) | *„Ismerős a készenléti üzemmód?"* / first-person: *„Éjjel is pörgött az agyam."* (Does standby mode sound familiar? / My brain was spinning at night too.) |
| *„Adósságban vagy?"* (Are you in debt?) | The founder's story about people who come to him in financial trouble, in the third person |
| *„Túlsúlyos vagy?"* (Are you overweight?) — ICP 3 | *„Stresszevés után mindig újrakezdtem"* (I always started over after stress eating) — first-person creator voice |

First-person creator statements and third-person stories are the safe pattern. The trigger-event hook ([[Hooks]] #22) is where this rule bites most.

## 3. Claims that are brand-stated (unverified) — status

| Claim | Status | Use |

| "Research at several US universities: motivational techniques are 97% ineffective, sometimes harmful" | Relayed from Loyd; **no study cited** | **Don't use as a stat** (`[STAT NEEDED — verify before publishing]`). The founder can say "the 97% figure Loyd cites" only if the source is found |
| "Alpha state is 8–14 Hz" | Commonly given as 8–12/13 Hz; roughly OK as an explanation | Fine in the founder's explanation; not as a claim of effect |

## 4. Offers and pricing

- **"Egyszeri befektetés, örök hozzáférés" (one-off investment, lifetime access)** is fine if true.
- **"Kiemelten kedvezményes jegyárak" (specially discounted tickets)** is fine if a real undiscounted price exists.
- **No fake reference prices.** This is a brain override ([[Scriptwriting]]).
- **Affiliate programme:** affiliate or creator content promoting the course must be **disclosed as advertising** (UCPD; Hungarian influencer guidance from the GVH, the Hungarian Competition Authority).
- **Scarcity for live events** ("only X seats left") must be true.

### Current struck-through prices to verify (EU Omnibus / HU price-reduction rule)

For **physical goods** (the books, the printed Guide), a crossed-out "was" price has to be the **lowest price in the prior 30 days** for that item. For **digital or service offers** (the Minikurzus, event tickets), the strict 30-day rule may not apply, but a fictitious reference price is still misleading under UCPD. Treat both the same way. This is the same rule as the brain's no-fake-reference-price override ([[Scriptwriting]]). As of 2026-09-01:
- Book 1: 6,590 → 5,995 Ft. Plausible, but verify the 30-day history.
- **Minikurzus: 35,990 → 11,977 Ft (−67%).** Was it ever sold at 35,990 in the last 30 days? This is the one most likely to fail.
- Guide: 4,999 (with Book 1) vs 4,800 (with Book 2). Two different "was" prices for the Guide; the expanded edition may explain it.
- **Book 2: 11,750 → 6,950 Ft.** It's a pre-order that has never been sold, so the 11,750 is the book plus the Guide's value, not a previous price. Frame it as *„6 950 Ft + ajándék Útmutató (4 800 Ft értékben)"* (6,950 Ft + a free Guide worth 4,800 Ft) instead of striking through a price the book never had.
- Event tickets: 79,995 → 59,995 with "Limitált idejű Ünnepi kedvezmény" (limited-time holiday discount) and "Utolsó férőhelyek" (last seats). Both must be true when the ad runs.

## 5. Tone rails

- Attacking the *motivation industry* is on-brand. **Attacking the viewer** isn't: no shame for being "lazy," no "you're weak." The founder's own frame is compassion: *„senkit nem szabad elítélni"* (no one should be judged).
- Don't name competitor gurus (Tony Robbins, Brian Tracy, Dispenza) in paid ads unless the quote is accurate and fair (comparative advertising rules). The founder's talks name them; ads should say "motivational speakers" or quote the belief, not the person.
- Religion: the "fear-based faith" teaching is usable. Never phrase it as a second-person assertion of the viewer's faith (Meta personal-attributes policy).
- Suicide and depression in a hook read as a mental-health treatment claim (section 1), so they stay out of hooks.

## 6. Checklist before any asset ships

1. No cure, treat or heal language, and no symptom promised as an outcome.
2. No second-person assertion of a health, mental-health, weight or financial status.
3. Every number has a source in the brain, or is flagged `[STAT NEEDED]`.
4. Testimonials are real, consented and dated; composite [C] lines are never quoted ([[Language Bank - Founder and VOC]]).
5. Family stories have consent on file.
6. Affiliate or creator content is disclosed.
7. The brain overrides still apply: Andromeda-distinct iterations ([[Making Iterations]]) and no fake "was" prices ([[Scriptwriting]]).
