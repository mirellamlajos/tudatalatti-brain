# The Method — Mechanism and Beliefs

This is the brand's belief system as Lajos Antal teaches it, extracted from the two event talks. For ads, this file is the **Unique Mechanism of the Problem and of the Solution** ([[Scriptwriting]] → "The Million-Dollar Mechanism") and the source of every myth-busting and contrarian hook ([[Hooks]] #13, #15).

**Framing rule:** these are the brand's teachings, not established science. In ads, attribute them ("the method teaches…", the founder's POV) and never present them as medical fact. See [[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]].

## The anchor quote

> *„A dolgok nem kívül kezdődnek, hanem belül, és nem alul, hanem felül, és nem a láthatóban, hanem a láthatatlanban."* — Hamvas Béla

The founder opens every talk with it. Things don't begin outside but inside, not below but above, not in the visible but in the invisible.

## The problem mechanism (UMP)

1. **The subconscious decides; the conscious mind barely does.** The subconscious runs ~90% of life, the conscious mind ~10% (brand-stated). It works 24 hours a day and controls cells, energy and reactions. Everyday proof: the forgotten word that pops up two days later, because the subconscious kept looking.
2. **Stress writes the program.** Nature's way of writing into the subconscious is **stress-induced fear**. The child touches the hot stove and never does it again. Useful for survival, but modern "civilisation stress" (the police car, the boss, the tax office) keeps writing fear-based programs. The result is **„félelem alapú programozottság"** (fear-based programming).
3. **The subconscious stores these as images, not words.** Children spend ~90% of their first 5–6 years in alpha, absorbing images.
4. **Stress causes an energy deficit.** In fight-or-flight the body shuts down "non-urgent" functions, including the immune system. Chronic stress fills the **„stresszhordó"** (stress barrel). When it overflows, energy drains, motivation disappears, and illness and financial collapse follow. *„Ha nincs elektromos energia, nincs világítás."* (No electricity, no light.)
5. **Bad traits are energy-deficit compensation.** Envy, malice, schadenfreude (*„Ha az én tehenem megdöglött, dögöljön meg a szomszédé is"*), hypocrisy, the big car, the gym body: all *„pótselekvés"* (substitute actions) for inner weakness. So you shouldn't judge the "lazy" person: *„nem lusta, szerencsétlennek nincs energiája"* (they aren't lazy, they have no energy).
6. **Key reversal:** *„Nem a negatív gondolataid miatt vannak félelmeid, hanem a félelmeid miatt vannak negatív gondolataid."* You don't have fears because of negative thoughts; you have negative thoughts because of your fears. This is the line that dismantles positive thinking.

## The solution mechanism (UMS): the levels

From the Healing Code event talk. The numbering follows the founder's own talk, which goes 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 (and later mentions a "6th level" of love-based faith).

1. **Clear the subconscious:** erase stress images with **Alexander Loyd's Healing Code** (*gyógyító kód*, *kódolás*, hand positions). It's simple and short, and needs no stepping out of the comfort zone.
2. **Energy returns → the child state.** The immune system and energy recover. You become like a child who doesn't need willpower or persistence to go and play: *„Kellett hozzá kitartás? Nem kellett."*
3. **Find your true inner goal** (*„igazi végső belső cél"*). It is never material; it's *nyugalom, béke, megelégedettség* (calm, peace, contentment). His "why would that be good for you?" drill: 1M Ft a month → pay the bills → no bailiff → *„mert akkor legalább nyugtom lenne"* (because then at least I'd have peace).
4. **Reconnect the subconscious with the universe:** unconditional forgiveness, a state of grace. Energy = *„a szeretet megnyilvánulása az emberben"* (love made manifest in a person). Envy and malice fall away.
5. **Only now send new information:** alpha state (8–14 Hz; closed eyes, half-dream, unfocused), messages as **images**, visualisation, Silva techniques. Then the subconscious's **„teremtő képesség"** (creative power) delivers, including the material goals, paradoxically, as a bonus.

**Order matters:** visualisation or Silva mind control on an uncleared subconscious fails, because the old program "kidobja" (throws out) and overwrites the new information, and there's no energy to realise it. Visible progress takes anywhere from *„egy hét"* (a week) to *„egy év"* (a year). Don't rush it and don't force it.

**The financial-crisis trap:** people arrive in money trouble and want an instant fix. The method works from the beginning (stress → energy → health), and money problems *„visszafejtődnek"* (unwind) later, often by an indirect route. Treating the method as a fix for tomorrow's money problem is why people say "it doesn't work."

### Official framing: the six levels of Subconscious Coding (Book 2 and the Minikurzus)

The website now presents the practice as **„A Tudatalatti Kódolás hat szintje"** (the six levels of Subconscious Coding), taught step by step with **kérésszövegek** (request texts). The levels it names are *törlés* (clearing) → *energetizálás* (energising) → *a végső belső célod megtalálása* (finding your ultimate inner goal) → …, each **tuned separately for health, relationships and financial success**. The talk's five steps above map onto this. The talk also mentioned a later "sixth level," love-based faith. **Use "six levels" in all public-facing copy**, and ask the brand for the exact names of levels 4–6.

**Recommended practice rhythm** (site FAQ): *„Napi 1–2 alkalommal, reggel és este"* (once or twice a day, morning and evening). Missing a session is fine, and breaks are allowed.

## The practice, as the team describes it (CEO walkthrough, 2026-09-25)

The CEO's plain-English version of the doctrine. All [Stated]; it matches the talks and adds the practical shape of the method.

- **The daily practice:** a Healing Code session is **about 7 minutes**, done **twice a day: after waking and before bed.** A few hand positions: the brand's own ad copy describes fingers pointed at certain points **on the face and neck, from 1–2 cm away, without touching, in a set order, while silently repeating a sentence** ([[Winners - Ad Library]] W-001; the CEO said "points on your head"). No breathing technique to master, no flexibility, "no weird stuff with your body." The team's name for the practice: **kódolás**.
- **No middleman:** no therapist, hypnotherapist or psychologist needed. You do it alone.
- **Built for the modern person:** no retreat, no monk mode in the hills, no two hours of yoga a day. For someone with a job, a family and responsibilities. This is the brand's speed and simplicity claim, and a natural permission hook ([[Hooks]] #21).
- **Why the mainstream fails (her framing):** Hill, Proctor and the rest stop at conscious decisions. What they give is "a momentary good feeling and a dopamine hit," not lasting change. The subconscious runs 90–95% of life; the conscious mind 5–10%.
- **The mechanism in one line:** the subconscious records every memory as an image; change the images and your reaction to the same situation changes. The goal isn't to fix the environment (you can't control it) but how you experience it inside.
- **Loyd as the authority:** the brand leans on Dr. Alexander Loyd's doctorate, US recognition, testimonials and "clinical studies" (his own; not independently checked here). Antal adopted the Healing Codes and extended them to other areas of life.
- **The three pillars, in her words:**
  - **Health:** chronic stress keeps the body in fight-or-flight, so it never gets to lower cortisol and go into repair mode. The method "gives the body a chance to relax" so its own healing can run. **The brand's rule of thumb: the body heals itself; nothing external heals it. Never "100% guaranteed."** Ad-safe wording: [[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]] §1.
  - **Relationships:** people blame the other gender or the environment ("you can't find a loyal, good partner these days"). The loop repeats because they don't value or love themselves first. Reprogram inside, and the reaction, then the relationship, changes.
  - **Wealth:** money fears and self-limits ("rich people must be thieves," "I don't deserve abundance") are absorbed from parents and society. They aren't yours. You can drop them the moment you see they're self-imposed.
- **The one limitation she names:** "not enough studies" and general scepticism. Her answer is 50,000+ copies and the daily flow of reader stories.

## Book 2 adds three theses (2026)

From the Book 2 product page ([[Website - Snapshot Sept 2026 (+ Jan 2026 event page)]]). They sharpen the mechanism for readers who "understand everything, yet nothing changes":

1. **„A tudatalattid nem szabotál, hanem véd."** Your subconscious doesn't sabotage you, it protects you. This reframes "self-sabotage" (ICP 2, the core avatar's pain) as protection, which is blame removal at the next level.
2. **„A felismerés még nem gyógyulás."** Realising something isn't yet healing it. Insight and resolve can't delete what stress memories guard; this answers the "I understand it all" plateau.
3. **„A változáshoz nem több erőfeszítés kell. Kevesebb."** Change doesn't need more effort; it needs less. The anti-willpower line, compressed.

Also new: **spiritual practices (the universe, karma, cards, angels, the "law of growth") are "pusztán mankók"** (mere crutches) that look for the answer outside. This is useful for ICP 5 believers, but handle it gently: it's contrarian toward their current beliefs ([[Hooks]] #15).

Book 2's cast turns the ICPs into characters: **„a bajnok Gábor"** (the champion who did everything right for 20 years, in vain), **„a halogató Feri"** (the procrastinator, whose answer is always "majd" — later) and **„a vállalkozó Emese"** (the entrepreneur who understands everything, yet nothing changes). ## The named false beliefs (tévhitek) the brand attacks

Each one is a ready myth-busting hook. The founder's own counter is given where he states one.

| False belief | Brand's counter |
|---|---|
| "Willpower can achieve anything" | Willpower only raises your stress level. It drains energy further and can end in depression. *„Az akaraterő elvárásokat ébreszt, újabb stresszt okoz."* |
| "Step out of your comfort zone" | Success is **inside** it. Step out and you step onto the path of failure. His favourite target. |
| "Positive thinking / swap your thoughts" | A painkiller at best (it works for 10 minutes). The fear stays. See the reversal above. |
| "Say 'I love myself' 100 (or 500) times" / affirmations | Speaking aloud puts the brain back in beta and closes the door to the subconscious. Words only reach the conscious 10%. |
| "Success is a decision" | *„Ha azt mondanám, hogy az egészség döntés kérdése, kinevetnétek."* (If I said health is a decision, you'd laugh.) |
| Law of Attraction / *The Secret* | "Doesn't exist in this sense." Outer goals roll off the subconscious *„mint a falra hányt borsó"* (like water off a duck's back). |
| Vision board on the fridge, "earn $1M a month" | Pure stress. It's written consciously, not in alpha, not as images, and the stress images aren't cleared. |
| "Burning desire" | You can't burning-desire your way to calm. It pulls you straight to the Audi A8. |
| "Strong faith moves mountains" | Distinguish **fear-based faith** ("God, just this once…", a sign of energy deficit), **willpower-based faith** (the Stallone revenge arc) and **love-based faith** (the only healthy kind, and it comes after clearing). |
| "Defeat is the gateway to victory" / *„Teher alatt nő a pálma"* | Defeat causes stress and fear. It pushes you further from victory. |
| "Sit in a luxury café with your last 100 Ft" | "Psychologically catastrophic." It generates more stress. |
| "Persistence / hard work" | Not needed when the goal matches your inner goal. The daughter and the book are his examples. |
| "Napoleon Hill (*Think and Grow Rich*)" | *„Már akkor sem voltak igazak, amikor leírta."* (They weren't true even when he wrote them.) |

**Money line for sceptics:** *„Mindent jól csináltok, csak amit csináltok, az nem jó, mert rosszak a tanácsadóitok."* You're doing everything right; what you're doing just isn't good, because your advisers are wrong. This removes the blame, which is exactly the "it's not your fault" move in the [[Scriptwriting]] Mechanism Builder.

## How this maps to ad mechanics

- **Mechanism, not motivation.** ICP 5 (the sceptic) and the core avatar both demand "mutasd meg, miért működik" (show me why it works). Lead with the mechanism: "not willpower, automatic programs."
- **Blame removal** converts high-sophistication buyers: "You're not lazy. You have no energy."
- **Visual metaphors ready to use** ([[Visuals]], "make the invisible visible"): the overflowing stress barrel, the door that closes when you speak, water off a duck's back, the battery or electricity with no light, the child running to the playground.
- **Contrarian fuel** ([[Hooks]] #13, #15): "STOP saying 'I love myself' in the mirror." "Step out of your comfort zone? That's exactly why it isn't working."
- **Emotional zone** ([[Hook Psychology]] #11, [[Emotional Delivery and Timing]]): the promise is *nyugalom* (calm), a Zone 1 (Soft) outcome. The founder's anger at the industry is a sanctioned Zone 3 dip (a shared frustration pointed at the industry, never at the viewer). This is a natural fit for the **permission hook** ([[Hooks]] #21): *„Nem kell kilépned a komfortzónádból."* (You don't have to step out of your comfort zone.)
