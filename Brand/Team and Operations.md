# Team and Operations

Everything here is [Stated] by the CEO in the 2026-09-25 walkthrough unless marked otherwise. Names not captured by the transcriber are marked **(name TBC)**.

## The team (6 people)

| Person | Role | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| **Lajos Mercédesz** ("Mercédesz", "M") | CEO and founder of the brand company; the older daughter of the author ([[Stories and Proof Assets]] row 3) | Creative strategy, copywriting, the brand's general direction. **Approves everything:** strategy, creative concepts, copy, design, campaigns. Scales the ads. Sets guidelines and priorities, delegates on Slack |
| **Mirella Lajos** ("Mirela") | Chief AI engineer | All systems and automation. Landing pages, website optimisation, anything technical on the front end. **Generates the ads with AI from Mercédesz's briefs.** "If she has more guidelines, she can execute anything" |
| **Customer-service manager (name TBC)** | Customer service agent and manager | Answers customers. Email marketing, builds the email flows. Helps with logistics: the fulfilment-centre ↔ Shopify connection, stock-keeping |
| **María** | Operations and process oversight | Makes sure logistics run on time. Coordinates with the book-printing companies. Part of finance; controls one of the company bank accounts |
| **Lajos Antal** ("Antal") | Author | Writes and develops the books' content. Some logistics with María. **Won't record to-camera video any more (as of Sept 2026)** |
| **István** | Media buyer (new hire) | The testing side of media buying (Mercédesz scales). Pulls swipe files from GetHookd and sets up the tests. Drives engagement and positive reactions on ads. Watches the brand's online presence: blogs and forums, what people say about the brand |

## How work flows

- **All communication runs on Slack.** Mercédesz directs, sets priorities, delegates.
- **Creative pipeline today:** Mercédesz decides the concept and copy → Mirella prompts and generates the ads with AI → Mercédesz approves → István tests → Mercédesz scales.
- **The fast lane that produced the current winner** ([[Walkthrough - Autoimmune Native Ad (2026-09-26)]]): István pulls English long-form ads into the GetHookd swipe file → Mercédesz and István translate and fit the book in → István launches from **Antal's verified Facebook page** → Mercédesz scales. Ads from Antal's page carry the most authority and scale furthest; whitelisted pages are the second option.
- **Approvals in the real world go to Mercédesz.** (The brain's "no approval gates" rule in `CLAUDE.md` is about the docs, not about live campaigns.)

## KPIs the brand runs on

> **Brain override — ROAS targets (Mercédesz, 2026-09-26).** The 2026-09-25 walkthrough said target ROAS 3 and break-even 1.8. The CEO corrected both the next day: **aim for at least 2.5 to scale, accept 2.0–2.2, break-even is 1.7.** The transcript stays as recorded; this table is the rule.

| Metric | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Scaling ROAS** | **2.5** | The ROAS the CEO wants to scale at. **2.0–2.2 is also acceptable.** 3–4 is "amazing," scale everything |
| **Break-even ROAS** | **1.7** | Corrected twice: 2 → 1.8 (2026-09-25) → **1.7** (2026-09-26). Use 1.7 |
| **Peak daily ad spend to date** | **3,000–4,000 EUR/day** | Never more. Goal: get back there, then beyond, with the UK and Spain added |
| **Tracking** | An Excel sheet, updated and reviewed **daily** | Wants help measuring Meta performance too (direct account access or a Google Sheet export) |
| **Decision window** | **4-day average** | Daily numbers are read, decisions use the 4-day average |
| **Day-of-week pattern** | Mon–Tue weakest, weekends strongest | Drives the launch cadence in [[Operating Cadence and AI Workflows]] |

Not yet on record: CPA, AOV, the split of revenue by product, and the ad account's age and gender breakdown. Those come from the export ([[Analyzing Ad Account Data]]).

**One real data point so far:** W-001 ([[Winners - Ad Library]]) spent €260 and returned €650 in 7 days, ROAS 2.5, on an engagement-retargeting audience built from the account's biggest past spenders (mostly video ads that spent tens of thousands). Small sample; it's the CEO's example of "scaling ROAS," not a benchmark yet.

## Channels and tools

- **Pages the ads run from:** Antal's verified page ("Lajos Antal Anthony") and at least one whitelisted persona page, **"Kiss Ildikó egészség"** ([[Swipe Sources - Competitor Native Ads Sheet]]).
- **Acquisition:** Meta ads are the main channel. Some people see an ad, then Google the brand. Friends and family referrals. (The transcript also says "call traffic", garbled; likely "cold traffic".)
- **Retention:** email campaigns and flows; the private Facebook group; the online course as the next step after the books.
- **Stack:** Shopify, a Hungarian fulfilment centre, Slack, GetHookd (ad research, swipe file, and its clone feature for image variations), Meta Ads, Excel or Google Sheets, **Higgsfield** (image upscaling and generation, Mirella), **Claude** (landing-page builds, Mirella), **GemPages** (landing-page hosting), Canva (static ad files). Book printing in Hungary; a Chinese printer is quoting for UK and Spain stock.

## Gaps the CEO named

1. **No strong systems or process.** Concepts, briefs, ads and iterations get made "in a very random way." Wants as much as possible automated and systemised with AI.
2. **Execution speed.** Mercédesz oversees everything, is often overwhelmed, and is slow to develop, decide and hand off. She needs a system that lets her outsource promptly.
3. **Ad production.** No system for creating ads, editing video or designing. **No in-house video editor.**
4. **Video content.** Founder-to-camera video worked, then faded, and there's no new founder footage. AI UGC hasn't worked so far. See [[Interpreted Data]] (2026-09-25).
