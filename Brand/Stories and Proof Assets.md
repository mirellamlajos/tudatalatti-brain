They're lived, specific and told in his voice: exactly the proof [[Killer Performance Ads]] ("AI-replication moat") and [[Advertising to Older Audiences]] (expert frame) value. Each needs the flags below cleared before it runs in paid media.

- **Customer photos holding the book** (the homepage testimonial carousel): native UGC-style statics are ready once consent is confirmed.
- **The Dr. Alexander Loyd endorsement quote** (authority proof; [[Hooks]] #19). Verify it was written for Book 1, and don't imply it covers Book 2.
- **A professional founder portrait** (black turtleneck, signature; grey suit for events): for the polished pole of the barbell.
- **The 4 successful-reader interviews** (Minikurzus bonus) and **3 live-call recordings**: likely the best testimonial video already filmed. Ask for access.
- **The Book 2 characters** (Gábor, Feri, Emese): storytelling assets that avoid consent issues entirely.
- **The founder-video library from 2023–2025:** to-camera clips and speech extracts that performed well before fatiguing (CEO, 2026-09-25). Antal won't record new to-camera video, so this library plus the event footage is the whole founder-video pool. Inventory it, then re-cut with new first-3-second openers ([[Making Iterations]], Andromeda rule) before writing it off.
