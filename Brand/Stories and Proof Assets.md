# Stories and Proof Assets

The founder's stories are the brand's strongest creative raw material. They're lived, specific and told in his voice: exactly the proof [[Killer Performance Ads]] ("AI-replication moat") and [[Advertising to Older Audiences]] (expert frame) value. Each needs the flags below cleared before it runs in paid media.
