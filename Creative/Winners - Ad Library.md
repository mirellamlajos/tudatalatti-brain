### The visual

Black-and-white, 1930s–40s look. A young woman in a floral dress stands with a hand on her hip; a man kneels beside her holding a baby; they're in rows of broad-leaf plants (tobacco), a small wooden farmhouse and trees behind. It reads as "your grandparents at your age," which is the hook's premise. Doesn't look like an ad. It is the source ad's own photo, upscaled by Mirella in Higgsfield. It was the only image tested with this copy. Next: variations via the GetHookd clone feature or Higgsfield, each different enough to count as a new opener.

### Anatomy (why it works, beat by beat)

1. **Generational then-vs-now open** (0–3 s): "Autoimmune diseases were almost unknown when your grandmother was your age. Today 1 in 12." A comparison hook ([[Hooks]] #17) that's about grandma, so it avoids the second-person attribute trap.
2. **Credibility stack:** four rising statistics in short lines. Same species, same genes → "it isn't your biology, it's how we live." Blame removal, early.
3. **"A hundred suspects… but one thing nobody talks about":** the unique mechanism of the problem ([[Scriptwriting]]): we lost the daily clearing of stress; the phone is a pocket alarm; "the first generation living with continuous unprocessed tension."
4. **Mechanism, "pure biology":** the subconscious stores every stress moment as an image → the alarm never switches off → cortisol stays high → the immune system can't regulate. This is [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]] compressed into six lines.
5. **First-person story:** "I'm 56." Three years of morning stiffness, swollen ankles, fatigue. Tests "fine," "it's just aging." Then the realisation: "I couldn't remember the last time I let myself stop."
6. **The villain:** "You can't patent a subconscious technique. No pill, no prescription, no monthly refill. Not because it's wrong. Because there's no money in it."
7. **Reveal:** the book by name; the technique is **kódolás**: fingers pointed at points on the face and neck, 1–2 cm away, no touching, in order, repeating a sentence. "Like jump-starting a dead battery. Physics."
8. **Results timeline with a sceptic witness:** night 1 nothing → a few mornings later she just sat up → week 1 no red sock line → week 3 the stiffness "went quiet" → week 5 the openly doubting husband says "you move like you used to" without looking up. Four months on, still doing it.
9. **Authenticity warning:** "incomplete, inaccurate versions circulate online; they skip the hand order, the distance, the relaxed state." Get the official material: book + Guide (with pictures) + Minikurzus, discounted now.
10. **Qualifying close:** "If your body feels heavier every year, if your results are always 'fine,' ask the one question they won't: did your grandparents have this at your age?" Link.
11. **P.S.:** "Normal and common are not the same thing."

**Spine to keep in iterations:** beats 3, 4, 6, 8 and 11. **Things to change for a new Entity ID:** the photo, the opening statistic, the narrator's age and sex, the symptom set, the witness.

### Flags against the wall ([[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]])

**Ruling (Mercédesz, 2026-09-26): this type of ad is in the acceptable zone; write more like it.** The table stays so iterations know which lines are guidance and which hit the hard floor (no cure, no guarantee, no doctor-bashing, no fake prices, sourced numbers). Against that floor, W-001 has two items to fix in the next version: the statistics need sources on file, and "senki nem sétál be az orvosod rendelőjébe… mert nincs benne pénz" should stay aimed at pharma, not the doctor.
