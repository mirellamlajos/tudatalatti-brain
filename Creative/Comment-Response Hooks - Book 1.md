# Comment-Response Hooks — Book 1

Format: [[Hooks]] #1. Every entry uses a real [R] comment that's in the consent log ([[Tribal Knowledge]] → Consent log), trimmed only per [[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]] §3c. The candidates and their ranking are in [[Review Context - Book Reviews, FB + Website (2026-09-28)]], hunt 2. Copy is Hungarian; the English in parentheses is a gloss, not a script.

Two rules that hold across every entry. The reply on a thread card is Antal's answer written for the ad and run from his own page; it's never presented as a screenshot of the original thread (his real replies were short thank-yous), and the original comment is never changed beyond a §3c trim. And every jab lands on the motivation industry, its books, its advice and its guarantees, never on the commenter, the viewer or doctors (§5).

Launch order by objection recurrence: H1, H2, H4, H3. All four are built.

## H4 · „Mindenkin nem fog működni" (the no-guarantee comment)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Source** | R15, I.S., Facebook comment under the author's post, ≈2026-03-19, 3 reactions ([[Reviews - Facebook Comments (Book)]]) |
| **Consent** | Cleared 2026-09-28, clause-gated: first sentence and the "well-organised book" clause only |
| **Objection it answers** | "Will it work for me / for everyone" (4 of 75 in the corpus). Scepticism is the CEO's #1 brake ([[Tribal Knowledge]]) |
| **Pairs with** | #1 comment response + #8 "how do I know if it's for me": the body is a who-it's-for frame |
| **Product** | Book 1 (+ Guide) |
| **Audience** | 50+ book buyer, high sophistication; cold and engagement retargeting both |
| **Launch pad** | Antal's verified Facebook page first ([[Tribal Knowledge]]: ads from it survive longest) |

**Why it works:** a commenter says the thing no ad can say, "no guarantee." Agreeing with him is the pattern interrupt. Then the honest expectation ("a week for some, a year for others") is the trust move this sceptic has never been given by a self-help ad. The sharp edge points at the guarantee-sellers, never at him and never at the viewer (§5).

### The on-screen comment (trimmed per §3c, original spelling kept)

> „Az nyílvávaló hogy mindenkin nem fog működni a dolog mivel mindannyian külömbözőek vagyunk…"
> — I. S. · 2026. március

Cut, and why: the „5-10%" clause (a number on an ad with no source, §3 and §6.3); the medication sentence (§1); the rest of the review. Show initials unless the consent covers the name. Keep his spelling. It's what makes it read as a real person and not as copy.

### Version A · Static thread card (no camera needed)

The founder answers comments in writing, in the te-form, short and warm, signed ☀️ ([[Reviews - Facebook Comments (Book)]]: *„Ha szükséges segítség írj nyugodtan."*). A card showing the comment and his reply looks exactly like the real thread, which is the most native comment-response static available to a brand whose founder won't record new video.

**Reply (founder, his written register):**

> „Igazad van. Nem is ígérem, hogy mindenkinél működik. Garanciát a motivációs előadók osztogatnak, én nem. Reggel hét perc, este hét perc, és a tested végre esélyt kap, hogy megnyugodjon. Kinél egy hét, kinél egy év. Ha elakadsz, írj nyugodtan ☀️"

(You're right. I don't promise it works for everyone. The motivational speakers hand out guarantees, I don't. Seven minutes in the morning, seven at night, and your body finally gets a chance to calm down. A week for some, a year for others. If you get stuck, write to me.)

**Layout (the W-002 family in the new identity, [[Winners - Ad Library]]):**

- Top two-thirds: a Facebook-style thread. Comment block: round avatar with "IS", name line "I. S.", the trimmed comment, the 3 reactions. Reply block, indented: Antal's photo as the avatar (one of the two visual anchors that keep working), "Lajos Antal · Szerző", the reply, ☀️.
- Bottom strip: Book 1 + Guide mockups joined by "+", the gold BEST SELLER rosette, the red→blue gradient.
- No headline above the thread. The thread is the hook. Optional small kicker: „Egy olvasó megkérdezte." (A reader asked.)
- **Andromeda variants** (each visibly different at first glance, so Meta counts them as new ads): V1 the thread on a plain white "screenshot" background with the book strip below. V2 the thread over the gradient with the founder portrait on the right. V3 the comment alone, big, in the W-002 headline slot, with the reply as the body copy under it.

### Version B · Native long-copy opener (primary text, Antal's page)

The first ~120 words. The body continues in the W-001 spine ([[Winners - Ad Library]]: mechanism, reveal, authenticity warning, close) written in experience words, without the health claims.

>
> És a tested végre esélyt kap, hogy megnyugodjon.
>
> Kinél egy hét. Kinél egy év.
>
> Kinek való, és kinek nem? Leírom őszintén.
>
> Annak való, aki már ezer módszert kipróbált, és még mindig úgy érzi, valami visszatartja. Annak, aki nem újabb elméletet akar, hanem valamit, amit holnap reggel elkezdhet. Annak, aki beéri azzal, hogy nyugodtabb lesz, és a többi jöhet utána.

(Gloss: This is what a reader wrote under the book. He's right. I don't promise it works for everyone. The motivational speakers hand out guarantees. I'm an engineer. What counts for me is what works, not what's promised. What I can say is this: seven minutes in the morning, seven at night. No willpower, no persistence, no stepping out of anywhere. And your body finally gets a chance to calm down. A week for some. A year for others. Who is it for, and who isn't it for? I'll write it honestly. It's for the person who's tried a thousand methods and still feels something holds them back. For the one who doesn't want another theory but something they can start tomorrow morning. For the one who'll settle for being calmer, and the rest can follow. It's not for the person who wants a guarantee. Not for the one who needs it by tomorrow. Whoever wants to understand everything first, the book is there for that. The seven minutes don't require understanding anything.)

**Then the body, in this order:** the mechanism in four lines (*„A tudatalattid minden stresszes pillanatot képként tárol el. Amíg a képek ott vannak, a riasztó nem áll le, akkor sem, ha épp semmi baj. A kódolás ezeket a képeket törli. Nem az akaratoddal: egy hétperces gyakorlattal, néhány kéztartással, csukott szemmel."*) → the book by name and the Guide with the hand positions in pictures → the authenticity beat from W-001 (incomplete versions circulate; get the official material) → the who-it's-for close repeated in one line → link. No cortisol, no immune system, no symptom list; the promise stays *nyugodtabb*.

### Version C · Spoken (video)

Who can deliver it: Mercédesz to camera (open with *„Ezt írta egy olvasó Antal könyve alá"*, then read his reply as his: *„Antal ezt írta vissza:"*), a 50+ reader creator in the first person, or the founder if he records. Szóval én csak annyit mondok. Reggel hét perc. Este hét perc. Nem kell hozzá akaraterő, nem kell kitartás, és a tested kap egy esélyt, hogy végre megnyugodjon. Kinél egy hét, kinél egy év. Ennyi. Ha elakadsz, írj nyugodtan.

(Someone wrote this under the book. That obviously it won't work for everyone. Well, he's right. I don't promise it. Whoever promises a guarantee is a motivational speaker, and I wrote a whole book about that not working. I'm an engineer. I too decided things for years, consciously, hard, and nothing came of it. So all I say is this. Seven minutes in the morning. Seven at night. No willpower, no persistence, and your body gets a chance to finally calm down. A week for some, a year for others. That's it. If you get stuck, write to me.)

**Body (20–45 s):** the who-it's-for block from Version B, spoken: *„Kinek való? Annak, aki már ezer módszert kipróbált… Kinek nem? Aki garanciát akar. Akinek holnapra kell."* Then the book and the Guide on screen, and the soft close: *„Nézd meg a könyvet. Ha nem neked való, azt is megírtam benne."* (Have a look at the book. If it's not for you, I wrote that in it too.)

**Read-aloud check:** breaths land unevenly (a long run-on, then "Ennyi."); one filler ("Hát,") and one turn ("Szóval") where the thought actually turns; "hét perc" repeated on purpose; no ellipsis pauses, no dash cadence, no "nem csak… hanem", no self-question in the body. If Mercédesz delivers, the "Én mérnök vagyok… nem lett belőle semmi" beat becomes third person about Antal or gets cut.

**Why it works:** the 10-of-75 buyer story, said by a real person with a number, before she'd read a page, so it can't be brand echo.
