# Angle Map — ICPs to Creative

The ICP research recommends **six creative universes**: separate visuals, vocabulary and promise for each. That's exactly the [[Meta Creative Diversity (Andromeda v2)]] rule: each persona gets visually distinct creative, so each universe gets its own Entity ID. This file turns the research into a plan the brain's methods can execute.

Across all of them, the default creator is **the founder** (see [[Creative Strategy by Brand Size]]: founder ads while the brand is at a relatable scale). Brief him loosely: he's an authority with his own voice ([[Creator Briefs]]).

## Funnel and format split

- **The brand's mechanism is a VSL natural** ([[Scriptwriting]], VSL section): named problem mechanism (stress images, energy deficit), named solution mechanism (clear → energise → inner goal → visualise), a product reveal (the book/course as the tangible delivery system), and a founder close. The event talks are already long-form leads.
- **50–60% of creative for unaware and problem-aware audiences** ([[Scriptwriting]]); the rest solution-aware and warm (book owners → course, [[New Product Launches]] warm-audience logic applies to the course as an upsell to readers).
- **Barbell** ([[Killer Performance Ads]]): super-raw founder and event clips on one end; clean, obvious-ad statics quoting one [F] line on the other.
- **Older tail (45–52+):** longer storytelling, big text, the expert frame, a native blog-review style ([[Advertising to Older Audiences]]).

## 🆕 Book 2 launch (pre-order live since ~2026-09-01)

Classified with [[New Product Launches]]:
- **Scope:** an established brand with a proven SKU (Book 1, 40k+ sold) launching a genuinely new product. In scope.
- **Audience fit:** an **existing persona**, specifically the slice who read Book 1 and got stuck at practice ("értem, mégsem változik" — I understand, yet nothing changes). That's the pattern repeated across ~75 real reviews and comments ([[Interpreted Data]]).
- **Type of newness:** the same product family, a new volume that serves a new use (from understanding to applying).
- **Result: Scenario B, Bridge & Expand.** The bridge is already written by the brand: *„Az első kötet megmutatta, hol a megoldás. Ez a kötet megmutatja, hogyan férsz hozzá."* (Book 1 showed where the solution is. This one shows how to reach it.)

| Phase | Audience | Awareness | Lead angle | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm recapture: now** | Book 1 buyers, the email list, the FB group, the event attendees | Most aware | *„Olvastad az elsőt? Ez onnan indul, ahol te elakadtál."* (Read the first? This starts where you got stuck.) The pre-order plus the free expanded Guide | Founder video "why I wrote volume 2"; a static with both covers (red + blue, the gradient); email + ad pairing |
| **Cold scaling: Q4 → January** | Core avatar, ICP 1, 2, 5 | Problem-aware | The title as the hook: *„Amikor már mindent értesz, mégsem változik semmi"*. Pair it with *„a tudatalattid nem szabotál, hanem véd"* as a myth-busting beat ([[Hooks]] #15) | Character stories (Gábor, Feri, Emese, [[Hooks]] #18); review-as-primary-text with W06/W07 ([[Customer Review Mining Method]]); the "halogató Feri" static for ICP 2 |
| **Bundle** | New cold buyers | Solution-aware | Book 1 + Book 2 as "understand → access" | [[New Product Launches]] bundle rule: lead with completeness, not "we have a bundle" |

**Launch proof** ([[New Product Launches]] Step 7: no reviews for Book 2 yet): use the founder's "why," inherited Book 1 proof clearly attributed ("40 000+ olvasó az első kötet után" — 40,000+ readers after the first volume), the Book 2 characters, and the "Magyar olvasói kódolás tapasztalatai" (Hungarian readers' coding experiences) bonus PDF. **Don't show Book 1's 4.9/5 or its Loyd quote as if they were about Book 2.**

**Pricing framing fix before ads:** show *„6 950 Ft + ajándék bővített Útmutató"* (6,950 Ft + a free expanded Guide) instead of a struck-through 11,750 ([[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]] §4).

**Iteration rule still applies:** every Book 2 variant needs a visibly different first 3 seconds (Andromeda override, [[Making Iterations]]).
