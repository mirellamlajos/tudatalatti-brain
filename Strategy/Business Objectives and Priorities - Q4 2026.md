# Business Objectives and Priorities — Q4 2026

All goals [Stated] by Mercédesz (CEO), 2026-09-25.

## Goals

**Primary**
1. **Scale as much as possible and acquire new customers.**
2. **A $1M revenue month in 2026.** The window: November into December, specifically **Black Friday → Cyber Monday → mid-December.**
3. **Expand beyond Hungary: the UK and Spain.**

**Secondary**
4. **Retention in Hungary:** book buyers → the online course.
5. **UK and Spain:** expand and scale as fast as possible once live.

## What the $1M month needs (Inferred)

Rough arithmetic so the plan is honest about the step-up. All inputs are the CEO's own numbers.

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue goal | $1,000,000 in roughly one month |
| Target ROAS | 3 |
| Implied paid spend at ROAS 3 | ≈ $333,000 |
| Over 30 days | ≈ $11,000/day |
| Best day ever | 3,000–4,000 EUR/day (≈ $3,500–4,700) |

So at target ROAS the month needs roughly **2.5–3× the best spend day the brand has ever had, sustained for a month**, unless a big share comes from email, the FB group and repeat buyers at no ad cost. Two of the three markets have never run an ad. The plan should say where each slice comes from: the HU bundle on cold traffic, the HU email list and group (Book 1 owners buying Book 2 and the bundle), the course upsell, and whatever the UK and Spain can add in their first weeks. Re-run this table once the account export shows real AOV and the current daily spend.
