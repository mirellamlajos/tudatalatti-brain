## 3. Sophistication stage

**Read: stage 4 moving into stage 5** for the self-help-experienced reader, which is most of the buyer base. Evidence: "tried many methods and they didn't work" is the dominant buyer story (~9 of 75 reviews: Silva, hypnosis, meditation, many books); the CEO says buyers have "tried many things and nothing stuck"; the strongest hook on record attacks a rival mechanism rather than making a promise.

What that means:
- **Bare promises don't land.** "A calmer life" alone is a stage-1 line in a stage-4 market.
- **The mechanism has to be in the argument, and it has to be enlarged:** not only *what* (the subconscious) but *why the order matters* (clear the stress images first, then send new images in alpha; visualising on an uncleared subconscious gets "thrown out") and *how little it asks* (about 7 minutes, twice a day, no therapist, inside the comfort zone).
- **Cold openings go to identification.** Book 2's title, *„Amikor már mindent értesz, mégsem változik semmi az életedben"*, is already a stage-5 line.
- **Data-limited:** no competitor scan has been run. A GetHookd pass on Hungarian self-development advertisers would confirm the stage. The UK and Spain are unread; expect the English-language market to sit at stage 5 on "subconscious" and "manifesting" claims until data says otherwise.

## 4. The one belief, per offer

Method: [[One Belief and the Ten Questions]]. The honest "only through": the Healing Code is Dr. Alexander Loyd's and is credited openly. What's the brand's own is **the six levels of Subconscious Coding in their order**, as Lajos Antal teaches them, and the materials that carry them. The exclusivity rests there.

| Offer | New opportunity | Desire | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Book 1** | Change starts by clearing the stress images the subconscious stores, *before* any goal-setting, positive thinking or visualising | A calmer, more content life that doesn't run on willpower | *Kódolás*: the six levels of Subconscious Coding, as the book teaches them |
| **Book 2** | Understanding was never the missing piece. The subconscious isn't sabotaging, it's protecting, and change takes less effort, not more | For what the reader already understands to finally show up in daily life | The six levels step by step with the request texts (*kérésszövegek*), in Book 2 and the expanded Guide |
| **BFCM bundle** (Book 1 + Book 2 + Guide) | The whole route in one place: where the solution is, how to reach it, and what to do each day | To stop collecting half-methods and have one complete one | The two books and the Practical Guide, read in order |
| **Online course** | Doing the practice with someone who can see whether it's being done right, until it's a habit | To keep it up and know it's being done properly | The course, with the live calls with Lajos |
| **Live event** | A full day of coding together in one room, led in person | To feel it, not only read it, and not alone | The shared Masterclass session at the event |

Gate check on each: the first slot is something this market hasn't been told by the motivational canon. If a new angle can't pass that test, it isn't ready.

Open items: the bundle price and dates; whether the course calls are weekly (site) or biweekly (CEO); whether a 2027 event is planned.

## 7. Removing the three objections

Method: redefinition in [[Belief Building in Long Copy]]. Counted facts only.

| Objection | Move | Facts on file |
|---|---|---|
| "It sounds strange or hard" | **Simplify** | About 7 minutes · twice a day, morning and evening · a few hand positions · 1–2 cm, no touching · six levels · done alone, no therapist · missing a session is fine (site FAQ) |
| "A book can't matter that much" | **Escalate** | The method teaches that the subconscious runs about 90% of daily life [brand teaching, attribute it]; years of effort on the right goals held back by one step never done first |
| "Why this price, why now" | **True reason first** | Book 2's launch; the BFCM bundle; a real event date. Anchor only on prices charged ([[Compliance and Claims Watchlist]] §4) |

The strangest idea in any piece is the hand-position practice and the word *kódolás*. Per the belief-chain audit, it arrives late, after the problem mechanism is accepted, in the same plain shape as what came before it, and next to something familiar (W-001 used jump-starting a dead battery).

## 8. Identification: roles and the primary image

- **Primary image:** the founder's face and the book cover. These are the two visual anchors the CEO says keep working. Intensify them; don't replace them.
- **Character roles to test (Inferred):** the calm one · the sensible one who doesn't fall for woo-woo ([R] W08's language) · the one who finally found what works and passes it on ([R] R03: the partner who took the book over).
- **Achievement roles to test (Inferred):** the grandparent the family comes to · the one who held everyone together and now has something for themselves.
- **Believability test for every visual:** people and rooms a 50–75-year-old Hungarian reader sees as their own or one step up. Kitchen table, garden, *nyaraló*. No glossy casting.

## 9. Tone that builds belief here

Understatement and deadly sincerity ([[Belief Building in Long Copy]], camouflage). The brand already has the material: it takes a week to a year; it isn't a fix for tomorrow's money problem; [R] R15's cleared line that it won't work for everyone. Saying these out loud is a brand position and a belief builder at once.

## 10. Open tests

Judged on cost per buyer over a 4-day average ([[Testing Doctrine and Specificity]]; [[Team and Operations]]).

1. **Bright side against failed solutions.** A calm-outcome opening against the "what didn't work" opening.
2. **Sample first.** A book extract or free chapter, asked for after the story, against straight-to-sale.
3. **Cold against retargeting** for the native storytelling format.
4. **Advertorial page against product page.**
5. **Identification opening against mechanism opening** on cold 50+.
