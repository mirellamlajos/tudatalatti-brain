# Interpreted Data

**Purpose:** What our data *means*: the conclusions and hypotheses we draw from numbers and research. Raw data and sources live in `Research/`; link to them from here.

## 2026-09-24 — First read of real customer comments

Source: [[Reviews - Facebook Comments (Book)]]. 15 Facebook comments, ≈ Oct 2025 – Mar 2026, hand-picked by the user, so likely positive-skewed. **Directional, not representative.**

1. **The positioning lands in customers' own words.** "Finally someone who doesn't preach positive thinking and 'attract it'" (R13) and "I've tried a thousand methods, something blocks me inside" (R10) match [[The Method - Mechanism and Beliefs]] and the core avatar. Confidence: medium. R13 may be the brand's own message echoing back.
2. **The biggest new insight: practice friction.** 3 of 15 readers say the codes are hard, or that they have to push themselves to practise regularly (R06, R11; R01 "every day" as the counter-case). The book creates belief; keeping up the practice is where people stall. **Hypothesis:** the online course's strongest angle for book owners is "you understood it, now let's make it a habit, with live help," not more theory. Test it on the warm audience (book buyers and the FB group).
3. **The book is read slowly and re-read.** "Stop, reflect, re-read" appears in 3 of 15. It's a reflective, identity-level read, not a quick tip book. Creative implication: show the *reading moment* (the pause, the underlining, the page turned back), not just the cover.
4. **Founder video → book is a real purchase path** (R10, R12). Supports founder-led organic and paid content as the front of the funnel.
5. **Word of mouth in couples** (R03, "my partner commandeered it"): a single instance, so a candidate for a gift or word-of-mouth concept, not yet a pattern.
6. **The most-reacted comment (R05, 103 reactions) is a whole-review story**: hesitation → five chapters at once → "my abilities were hidden inside me" → noticing the beautiful again. That community resonance is the best signal in the set for a review-as-primary-text ad (with consent, sadness framed as outlook, no depression claim).

## 2026-09-24 — Combined read: Facebook comments + website reviews (≈75)

Sources: [[Reviews - Facebook Comments (Book)]] (15, dated) and [[Reviews - Website (Book)]] (~60, undated, 66 of 67 are 5★). **Both are positive-selected.** They're good for *language* and *themes*, useless for measuring satisfaction.

1. **"Tried many methods, and they didn't work" is the dominant buyer story** (~9 of 75). They've done Silva, hypnosis, meditation, theology, and many self-help books. **This confirms HIGH market sophistication:** agitate failed solutions and lead with the mechanism ([[Scriptwriting]]).
2. **Practice friction is now a confirmed pattern** (5 of 75: FB R06, R11; W13, W14, W15). **Book 2's title and positioning target exactly this plateau**: *„Amikor már mindent értesz, mégsem változik semmi"* (when you already understand everything, yet nothing changes). The brand's product direction and its customers' words agree. Hypothesis: Book 2 converts best on Book 1 owners first (warm), then as a cold problem-aware hook.
3. **"Nyugodtabb" (calmer) is the brand's real outcome word** (5+ independent uses). It matches the method's promised inner goal (*nyugalom*, calm) and is claims-safe. **Hypothesis:** ads that promise "calmer, less worried" will outperform ads that promise success or money. Test it against universe 6 (the money ceiling).
4. **Sceptic → convert and anti-woo language is real** (4 of 75). ICP 5 sceptics are a validated segment with their own verbatims (W07, W08).
5. **Acquisition runs through the founder and word of mouth.** Buyers mention the FB group, YouTube videos, a friend's recommendation, and a gifted copy (4 of 75). Founder content and gifting are both evidenced.
6. **Diaspora buyers exist** (DE, AT and SK flags). The HU-abroad geo targeting in the avatar is supported.
7. **Evidence that we're missing negatives:** only one 4★ and one delivery complaint are visible. Pull retailer reviews (Moly.hu, Libri, Bookline) and ad comments for objections before building comment-response hooks ([[Hooks]] #1).

**What would change these reads:** negative or neutral comments, retailer reviews (Moly.hu, Libri, Bookline), course and event feedback, and ad-account data (comment sentiment on ads, demographics by spend).
